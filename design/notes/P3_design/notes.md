# Significant Decisions

One decision I made that I thought was really funny was about deciding on the length of the negotiation. I reuse the proposing concept, which I think is quite funny. I wanted the users to have no way to actually meaningfully communicate (since, if there was a text chat, I had no guarantee that they wouldn't start negotiating prematurely), and so I needed a mechanism through which they could silently agree on a negotiation length without having a way to communicate extra information.
