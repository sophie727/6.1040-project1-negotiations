Nadia is a negotiation student who needs practice. A year after her negotiations class, her skills have gone rusty, and she has just accepted a job offer without negotiating it at all. She wants to get back into practice, but she's in a new city and doesn't know anyone to practice with. So she makes an account on **The Whole Pie**.

She's in a new city, so she doesn't have any negotiation friends. But she doesn't want to negotiate with an AI, either, so she presses "Someone new" -- to negotiate with someone new -- and then "Surprise me!" because she doesn't want to choose which negotiation to do.

The next week, none of her friends are free, so Nadia picks _Someone new_ and _Surprise me!_ for three people. She gets matched with Bob and Sophie, and the three of them go forward with a three-person negotiation, "The Launch Room". Nadia loves negotiating with Sophie, but Bob was terribly uncooperative. In the end, they barely get a deal through, because Bob spent the whole time hard bargaining. As a result, Nadia feels no qualms rating Bob awfully afterwards.
