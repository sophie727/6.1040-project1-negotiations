# Things I want to implement in the future (but not now)

- making it so that the app can update the users on the scenario halfway through the negotiation (e.g. halfway through the users are told "this bill just got passed" or "your friend just told you secretly that xyz is true"). It would probably be a popup overlaying the screen, and then after the pop up it goes at the end of the confidential brief.
- weighting ratings by the kind of negotiation, once ratings exist. Some cases are mostly about claiming value (buying a house: there isn't much value to create, and the relationship barely matters), while others are about creating value and keeping the relationship (a joint venture). Each case could say how much claiming, creating, and the relationship matter, so a game affects each of the user's ratings in proportion: a claim-focused negotiation moves the claim rating more than the create rating.

- I think instead of "a negotiation case can't change after you publish a negotiation", it should be "it can't be changed after someone starts to PLAY on your negotiation." But I think we can make this change later.
