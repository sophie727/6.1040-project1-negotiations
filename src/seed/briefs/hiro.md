# Confidential brief: Hiro

## The situation

You are Hiro, and for the past five weeks you've been interviewing with Fernwood Analytics, a mid-sized data company, to lead its client operations team. Last week, Manon, Fernwood's head of operations, told you that you're the top choice for the role. Today the two of you are meeting to settle the terms of your offer.

Five things are on the table, and both of you know the usual range for each. For a role like this in your city, salaries run from $80K to $120K. Employers offer anything from no health insurance to half or full coverage. First-year bonuses run from nothing to $10K. A new hire usually starts somewhere between January 1 and March 1, and the role could carry the title of assistant manager, senior manager, or director.

## Your priorities

Salary matters most to you. You're still paying off student loans, and a salary is the one number you can count on every year. Health insurance comes next: you have a chronic condition that needs regular care, so full coverage would make a real difference, and half coverage would still help.

You'd rather start on March 1. You promised your current team you'd see a project through to the end of February, and leaving early would mean breaking that promise. A stronger title would help your career, but it matters less to you than the money. A bonus would be nice, but bonuses depend on how the year goes, so you don't count on one.

You aren't out of options. Granite & Co. has offered you $100K, full health coverage, no bonus, and the title of senior manager, starting March 1.

## Your points

Your score is the total points of the agreement you reach. On salary, bonus, and start date, values in between earn points in between: a salary of $100K is worth 300 points to you, and a February 1 start is worth about 158.

| Issue            | Option            | Your points |
| ---------------- | ----------------- | ----------- |
| Start date       | January 1         | 0           |
|                  | March 1           | 300         |
| Salary           | $80K              | 0           |
|                  | $120K             | 600         |
| Health insurance | Full Coverage     | 300         |
|                  | Half Coverage     | 150         |
|                  | Not included      | 0           |
| Bonus            | $0                | 0           |
|                  | $10K              | 100         |
| Position title   | Assistant manager | 0           |
|                  | Senior manager    | 100         |
|                  | Director          | 200         |

Your BATNA, your best alternative to a negotiated agreement, is Granite & Co.'s offer, which is worth 1,000 points to you. Don't accept any agreement worth less than that: you'd be better off taking the other offer.
