# Confidential brief: Manon

## The situation

You are Manon, the head of operations at Fernwood Analytics, a mid-sized data company. For the past five weeks you've been interviewing Hiro to lead your client operations team, and last week you told him he's your top choice for the role. Today the two of you are meeting to settle the terms of his offer.

Five things are on the table, and both of you know the usual range for each. For a role like this in your city, salaries run from $80K to $120K. Employers offer anything from no health insurance to half or full coverage. First-year bonuses run from nothing to $10K. A new hire usually starts somewhere between January 1 and March 1, and the role could carry the title of assistant manager, senior manager, or director.

## Your priorities

Timing matters most to you. Fernwood is launching its biggest client of the year in January, and your team is already short-staffed. Every week without a leader in place costs you, so a January 1 start would be ideal, and a March 1 start would be your worst case.

You also have a budget to protect: the lower the salary, the better. You'd rather not pay a bonus either, though a bonus is a smaller, one-time cost. Health insurance makes no difference to you, because it's paid from Fernwood's company-wide benefits budget, not your team's.

You'd actually like the new hire to have a higher title. You're reorganizing the department, and a director could lead the client team and speak for Fernwood with clients. Director would be best, and senior manager would still be better than assistant manager.

You do have a fallback. Your second-choice candidate could start on January 1 as a senior manager, but would want $120K and no bonus.

## Your points

Your score is the total points of the agreement you reach. On salary, bonus, and start date, values in between earn points in between: a salary of $100K is worth 250 points to you, and a February 1 start is worth about 380.

| Issue            | Option            | Your points |
| ---------------- | ----------------- | ----------- |
| Start date       | January 1         | 800         |
|                  | March 1           | 0           |
| Salary           | $80K              | 500         |
|                  | $120K             | 0           |
| Health insurance | Full Coverage     | 0           |
|                  | Half Coverage     | 0           |
|                  | Not included      | 0           |
| Bonus            | $0                | 100         |
|                  | $10K              | 0           |
| Position title   | Assistant manager | 0           |
|                  | Senior manager    | 100         |
|                  | Director          | 200         |

Your BATNA, your best alternative to a negotiated agreement, is hiring your second-choice candidate, which is worth 1,000 points to you. Don't accept any agreement worth less than that: you'd be better off hiring the other candidate.
